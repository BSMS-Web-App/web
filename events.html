Halloween Haunt Is Coming!

Get ready for the ultimate Halloween candy-collecting adventure on BSMS Web! Halloween Haunt is an upcoming limited-time event where every house could hold a surprise, every neighborhood has something new to discover, and every piece of candy brings you one step closer to the top.

Explore Spooky Neighborhoods

Venture through Halloween-themed neighborhoods, explore streets lined with decorated houses, and see what surprises are waiting behind each door. The further you progress, the more exciting your candy-hunting adventures can become.

Ring the Doorbell. Grab the Candy.

Visit houses, ring their doorbells, and get ready for a candy-collecting experience. Discover different types of candy, chase rare finds, and work toward building an impressive collection. You never know what you might find!

Build Your Candy Empire

Collect candy, unlock new possibilities, upgrade your progress, and build your very own Trick-or-Treat Crew. Your crew will help you collect even more candy while you explore the event's features.

Discover Rare Treats

Not all candy is created equal! Keep an eye out for uncommon and extraordinary discoveries. Every candy type has a place in your collection, and rare finds will make your Halloween adventure even more memorable.

Climb the Leaderboard

Think you can collect enough candy to reach the top? Watch the leaderboard, keep improving your strategy, and see how you stack up against other players once the event begins.

When Does It Begin?

Halloween Haunt begins Monday, October 26, 2026, and ends Friday, October 30, 2026, at 3:10 PM EDT. This is a limited-time event, so make the most of it while it lasts!
